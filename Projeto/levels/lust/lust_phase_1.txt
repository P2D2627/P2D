Lust, phase 1 (level 1): one continuous map, climbing in a zigzag. Each part keeps its lesson on the dogs' floor; 1.2, 1.3
and 1.5 also have a high way; two cave networks to the east split and join again. The crucifix corridors of 1.4 and 1.7
have no way round. One character is one 48 px tile; the first grid line is the top row. Build the scene with
levels/text_map/build_map.gd. # rock, . air, P start and restart spot, F where phase 2 goes on, > < a dog's post and the
way it faces, + the crucifix mural; P, F, > and < stand on the tile below them. Section, shaft and chimney lines: name,
first column, first row, last column, last row. A shaft gets a camera zone; a chimney, inside a part, does not.
